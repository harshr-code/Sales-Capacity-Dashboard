Lead sufficiency & SC utilisation

SC count (Active) = people in the selected month's manpower file (Department = selected sales channel, city from the file) with status Active or Resigned, who also appear as SC on the MS to MD tab that month; In Training / Newly Hired are not counted. Working days = days in the selected range up to the as-of date, minus Mondays (weekly off). Meeting capacity = SC count × meetings per SC per day × working days. MD = all meetings done; MD / SC = MD ÷ SC count. Capacity utilisation = MD ÷ meeting capacity. MS required at current MS → MD = meeting capacity ÷ actual MS → MD, i.e. the MS needed to fill SC capacity at today's conversion; MS gap = MS required − MS to date (+ means more MS needed). Channel and sub-channel filters apply to MS and MD; capacity stays at city level.

Notes and definitions

Data sources
MS to MD tab (one row per lead: first meeting scheduled date, last meeting done date, current SC email, Field / Inside Sales, lead score, channel, sub-channel); BQL tab (BQL and leads sent to LRM by day, city, channel, sub-channel, lead score); MOP tab (day-wise MOP by city and channel); monthly manpower mapping (SC list).
Refresh
The data pulls from the Google Sheet automatically every 2 hours between 08:00 and 22:00 IST (GitHub may run these a little late). Numbers run up to the previous day (as-of date). The ↻ Refresh button loads the latest pulled data without losing filters. To force a fresh pull from the sheet immediately, the dashboard owner can run it here: Run refresh → Run workflow (about 3 minutes), then press ↻ Refresh.
Cities
All leads in the source are counted. Main cities (24): Delhi, Ghaziabad, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ahmedabad, Surat, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Amravati, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Nashik, Pune, Kolhapur, Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, Jabalpur, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Varanasi, Chennai. Other cities = every other city in the source (e.g. Agra, Meerut, Kota, Coimbatore, Jodhpur, Bareilly, Jalgaon, Raipur, Solapur, Vijayawada, Prayagraj, Trichy, Salem, Madurai, Visakhapatnam, Erode) plus blank / invalid city names, so PAN equals the sheet total. MOP comparisons cover the main cities only.
Channels
Digital — Digital sources only (Google, Meta, organic, inbound and other digital sub-sources).
Referral — referral leads excluding BTL-sourced referrals.
BTL — BTL activities, including BTL Referral.
SolarPro — SolarPro partner leads (Lead Model, Sales Model).
Others — all sources other than Digital, Referral, SolarPro and BTL: Cold Marketing / Discom data, newspaper, Experience Centre, Affiliate, IVR and untagged.
Channel and sub-channel are applied the same way in the BQL and MS to MD tabs.
Funnel stages
BQL = SUM(bill_qualified). LS LRM = leads sent to LRM, SUM(lead_delivered_to_lrm), shown for Digital only, as other channels' leads reach LRM without bill qualification. MS = meeting scheduled, counted once per lead on its first meeting scheduled date; MD = meeting done, counted once per lead on its last meeting done date. Effort basis: each stage is counted on its own date.
SC count and capacity
For the selected month's manpower file: (1) Department — Field sales or Inside sales, per the sales channel filter; (2) City — from the file; (3) Status — Active, Resigned, or In Training / Newly Hired. SC count (Active) = Active + Resigned people who also appear as SC (Col E) on the MS to MD tab in that month. In Training / Newly Hired are not counted. Anyone owning meetings who is not in the file, or has another status (No Update, Sabbatical, To be found), is Other SC. Each month uses its own file; resigned SCs count for the full month. Working days = days in the range up to the as-of date minus Mondays (weekly off); the filter bar shows the number of Mondays. Meeting capacity = SC count × meetings per SC per day (default 2, editable) × working days. MD / SC = total MD ÷ SC count. Capacity utilisation = total MD ÷ meeting capacity. MS required at current MS → MD = meeting capacity ÷ MS → MD.
Lead score bands (LQI)
Excellent 9–10 · Good 7–9 · Average 5–7 · Poor below 5 (a score of 9 falls in Good, 7 in Average). Leads without a score count under All only.
Colour rules
vs MOP / capacity / target: green ≥ 100%, amber 80–100%, red < 80%. Conversion rates: green / red when ≥ 5pp above / below the PAN rate for the same channel.
