Notes and definitions

Data sources and refresh
MS to MD tab (one row per lead: last meeting scheduled date, last meeting done date, current SC email, Field / Inside Sales, lead score, channel, sub-channel); BQL tab (BQL by day, city, channel, lead score); MOP tab (day-wise MOP by city and channel); monthly manpower mapping (SC list). Refreshed every 2 hours, 08:00–22:00 IST. Numbers run up to the previous day (as-of date); today's meetings show as booked ahead.
Refresh
The data pulls from the Google Sheet automatically every 2 hours between 08:00 and 22:00 IST (GitHub may run these a little late). The ↻ Refresh button loads the latest pulled data without losing filters. To force a fresh pull from the sheet immediately, the dashboard owner can run it here: Run refresh → Run workflow (takes ~3 minutes), then press ↻ Refresh.
Coverage
All leads in the source are counted. Main cities (24): Delhi, Ghaziabad, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ahmedabad, Surat, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Amravati, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Nashik, Pune, Kolhapur, Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, Jabalpur, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Varanasi, Chennai. Other cities = every other city in the source (e.g. Agra, Meerut, Kota, Coimbatore, Jodhpur, Bareilly, Jalgaon, Raipur, Solapur, Vijayawada, Prayagraj, Trichy, Salem, Madurai, Visakhapatnam, Erode) plus blank / invalid city names, so PAN equals the sheet total. MOP comparisons cover the main cities only.
Channels
Digital — Digital sources only (Google, Meta, organic, inbound and other digital sub-sources).
Referral — referral leads excluding BTL-sourced referrals.
BTL — BTL activities, including BTL Referral.
SolarPro — SolarPro partner leads (Lead Model, Sales Model).
Others — all sources other than Digital, Referral, SolarPro and BTL: Cold Marketing / Discom data, newspaper, Experience Centre, Affiliate, IVR and untagged.
Channel and sub-channel are applied the same way in the BQL and MS to MD tabs, so BTL Referral sits under BTL for BQL, MS and MD.
MS and MD
Effort basis: MS counted on the meeting slot date, MD on the meeting done date. The source holds the last meeting per lead, so a lead rescheduled more than once counts once, on its latest date. MS → MD % = MD ÷ MS for the same dates.
SCs
From the monthly manpower mapping, matched on SC email. Field Sales = Department Field sales, Designation SC / Sr SC; Inside Sales = Department Inside sales, Designation SC. Active = 30+ days tenure on the 1st of the month; In training = under 30 days; Resigned = status Resigned. Each month uses its own mapping.
Other SC and No SC
Other SC = meetings on leads whose SC email is not in that month's manpower mapping as an SC — TLs, Experience Centre staff, KAMs, ex-employees. No SC = lead with no SC email. Both are included in all MS / MD totals and excluded only from MD per SC.
Capacity and targets
Meeting capacity = SCs counted × meetings per SC per day × working days (days × working days per week ÷ 7). Defaults: 2 meetings per day, 6-day week, Active SCs only (In-training and Resigned SCs can be included) — all editable in the filter bar. MD/SC target = meetings per SC per day × working days elapsed.
Lead score bands
Excellent 9–10 · Good 7–9 · Average 5–7 · Poor below 5 (a score of 9 falls in Good, 7 in Average). Leads without a score count under All only.
Colour rules
vs MOP / capacity / target: green ≥ 100%, amber 80–100%, red < 80%. MS → MD %: green / red when ≥ 5pp above / below the PAN rate for the same channel.
