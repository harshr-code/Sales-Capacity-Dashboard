1 · Lead sufficiency — SC capacity vs meetings scheduled

SCs from the monthly manpower mapping (Field Sales: SC / Sr SC; Inside Sales: SC). Active = 30+ days tenure on the 1st of the month; In training = under 30 days; Resigned = status Resigned (never counted in capacity). Capacity = SCs counted × meetings per SC per day × working days; across months, each month uses its own SC count. MS counted on meeting slot date up to the as-of date; slots after it shown as booked ahead. Channel filter changes MS only — capacity stays at city level.

2 · MS → MD and SC utilisation

Selected sales channel, all lead channels, effort basis (MS on slot date, MD on done date, both within the selected dates up to the as-of date). Target MD/SC = meetings per SC per day × working days elapsed. MD/SC = MD by counted SCs ÷ SCs counted. MD is split by who did it, matched on the lead's SC email (Col E) to that month's manpower mapping: Other SC = email not in the mapping as an SC (TLs, Experience Centre, KAMs, ex-employees); No SC = blank email.
