Notes and definitions

Data sources
Raw Data tab (one row per lead): city (B), BQL flag (C), channel (D), sub-channel (E), lead score (F), first sales channel (G — splits BQL, LS LRM, MS and MD into Field / Inside), created date (I — BQL date), LRM date (J), first meeting scheduled date (K), first meeting done date (L), SC email (O). MOP tab (day-wise MOP by city and channel). Monthly manpower file (SC list). Rows without a Lead ID are counted, since each row carries its own dates.
Refresh
The dashboard pulls from the Google Sheet automatically through the day (08:00–22:00 IST). ↻ Refresh pulls the latest sheet data on demand: it republishes the sheet, rebuilds the dashboard and updates this page in about 3–5 minutes, keeping your filters (one refresh at a time, at most every 10 minutes). Numbers run to the last complete day in the sheet; a day the sheet has only partly loaded is held back.
Cities
The dashboard covers 24 cities: Delhi, Ghaziabad, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ahmedabad, Surat, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Amravati, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Nashik, Pune, Kolhapur, Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, Jabalpur, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Varanasi, Chennai. PAN = total of these 24 cities. Leads and SCs in any other city (e.g. Agra, Meerut, Kota, Coimbatore, Jalgaon, Solapur) are not included.
Channels
Digital — Digital sources only (Google, Meta, organic, inbound and other digital sub-sources).
Referral — referral leads excluding BTL-sourced referrals.
BTL — BTL activities, including BTL Referral.
SolarPro — SolarPro partner leads (Lead Model, Sales Model).
Others — all sources other than Digital, Referral, SolarPro and BTL: Cold Marketing / Discom data, newspaper, Experience Centre, Affiliate, IVR and untagged.
Channel = Col D and sub-channel = Col E of the Raw Data tab; Referral leads with sub-channel BTL count under BTL as BTL Referral.
Funnel stages
BQL = leads with BQL_Flag = 1, on their created date. LS LRM = leads sent to LRM, on the LRM date; shown for Digital only. MS = first meeting scheduled date; MD = first meeting done date — each lead counts once. Effort view (all tabs except BQL cohort): every lead's stage is counted if its date falls in the selected dates. BQL cohort: only BQL leads created in the selected dates, followed through LRM, MS and MD by month (M0 / M1 / M2); the LMTD cohort (same days last month) is shown to date in the table, and at the same age in the summary line.
SC count and capacity
From the selected month's manpower file: SC count (Active) = Field sales people with Designation SC or Sr SC, or Inside sales people with Designation SC, whose Current Status is Active, in their city from the file. Resigned, In Training / Newly Hired and other statuses are not counted. Each month uses its own file. Working days = days in the range up to the as-of date minus Mondays (weekly off); the filter bar shows the number of Mondays. Meeting capacity = SC count × meetings per SC per day (default 2, editable) × working days. MD / SC = total MD ÷ SC count. Capacity utilisation = total MD ÷ meeting capacity. MS required at current MS → MD = meeting capacity ÷ MS → MD.
Lead score bands (LQI)
Excellent 9–10 · Good 7–9 · Average 5–7 · Poor below 5 (a score of 9 falls in Good, 7 in Average). Leads without a score count under All only.
Colour rules
vs MOP / capacity / target: green ≥ 100%, amber 80–100%, red < 80%. Conversion rates: green / red when ≥ 5pp above / below the PAN rate for the same channel.
