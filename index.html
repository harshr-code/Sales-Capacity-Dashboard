Lead sufficiency & SC utilisation

SC count (Active) = Active + Resigned SCs from the monthly manpower mapping for the selected sales channel (Field Sales: SC / Sr SC; Inside Sales: SC); in-training SCs (under 30 days on the 1st) are not counted. Meeting capacity = SC count × meetings per SC per day × working days elapsed. MS on meeting slot date and MD on meeting done date, within the selected dates up to the as-of date. MD = all meetings done; MD by SCs = MD done by the SCs in the SC count; MD / SC = MD by SCs ÷ SC count; target MD / SC = meetings per SC per day × working days elapsed. Actual MS → MD uses all MS and MD. Channel and sub-channel filters apply to MS and MD; capacity stays at city level.

Notes and definitions

Data sources
MS to MD tab (one row per lead: last meeting scheduled date, last meeting done date, current SC email, Field / Inside Sales, lead score, channel, sub-channel); BQL tab (BQL and leads sent to LRM by day, city, channel, sub-channel, lead score); MOP tab (day-wise MOP by city and channel); monthly manpower mapping (SC list).
Refresh
The data pulls from the Google Sheet automatically every 2 hours between 08:00 and 22:00 IST (GitHub may run these a little late). Numbers run up to the previous day (as-of date). The ↻ Refresh button loads the latest pulled data without losing filters. To force a fresh pull from the sheet immediately, the dashboard owner can run it here: Run refresh → Run workflow (about 3 minutes), then press ↻ Refresh.
Cities
All leads in the source are counted. Main cities (24): Delhi, Ghaziabad, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ahmedabad, Surat, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Amravati, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Nashik, Pune, Kolhapur, Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, Jabalpur, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Varanasi, Chennai. Other cities = every other city in the source (e.g. Agra, Meerut, Kota, Coimbatore, Jodhpur, Bareilly, Jalgaon, Raipur, Solapur, Vijayawada, Prayagraj, Trichy, Salem, Madurai, Visakhapatnam, Erode) plus blank / invalid city names, so PAN equals the sheet total. MOP comparisons cover the main cities only.
Channels
Digital — Digital sources only (Google, Meta, organic, inbound and other digital sub-sources).
Referral — referral leads excluding BTL-sourced referrals.
BTL — BTL activities, including BTL Referral.
SolarPro — SolarPro partner leads (Lead Model, Sales Model).
Others — all sources other than Digital, Referral, SolarPro and BTL: Cold Marketing / Discom data, newspaper, Experience Centre, Affiliate, IVR and untagged.
Channel and sub-channel are applied the same way in the BQL and MS to MD tabs.
Funnel stages
BQL = SUM(bill_qualified). LS LRM = leads sent to LRM, SUM(lead_delivered_to_lrm), shown for Digital only, as other channels' leads reach LRM without bill qualification. MS = meeting scheduled (slot date), MD = meeting done (done date). Effort basis: each stage is counted on its own date. The source holds the last meeting per lead, so a lead rescheduled more than once counts once, on its latest date.
SC count and capacity
From the monthly manpower mapping, matched on SC email. Field Sales = Department Field sales, Designation SC / Sr SC; Inside Sales = Department Inside sales, Designation SC. SC count (Active) = Active + Resigned SCs in that month's mapping; SCs under 30 days' tenure on the 1st of the month (in training) are excluded. Meeting capacity = SC count × meetings per SC per day × working days (days × working days per week ÷ 7); defaults 2 per day and a 6-day week, editable in the filter bar. MD / SC = MD done by the counted SCs ÷ SC count. MD by TLs, Experience Centre staff, KAMs, in-training SCs and others stays in the MD total and MS → MD, but not in MD / SC.
Lead score bands (LQI)
Excellent 9–10 · Good 7–9 · Average 5–7 · Poor below 5 (a score of 9 falls in Good, 7 in Average). Leads without a score count under All only.
Colour rules
vs MOP / capacity / target: green ≥ 100%, amber 80–100%, red < 80%. Conversion rates: green / red when ≥ 5pp above / below the PAN rate for the same channel.
